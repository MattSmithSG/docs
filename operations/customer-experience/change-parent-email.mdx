How to change a customer's email that is associated with their account on the FKL website.

**Who:** Event Coordinator, Sales Team

## Prerequisites

- Access to the [Operator Dashboard (OD)](https://admin.uk.fat-kartingleague.com/)

## Procedure

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Operator Dashboard">
    Navigate to the [Operator Dashboard](https://admin.uk.fat-kartingleague.com/) and log in.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the customer's hub">
    From the dashboard, select the hub the customer is associated with.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to Users">
    Select **Users** from the top navigation row.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Search for the customer">
    Select **Free search**, then enter the customer's name or email address to pull up their profile.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the customer's profile options">
    Locate the customer in the results and select the **three dots (⋮)** icon on their profile.

    ![Three dots menu on a customer profile](/images/operations/customer-experience/change-email-three-dots.png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the new email address">
    Enter the new email address the customer wants to use.

    ![Change email input field](/images/operations/customer-experience/change-email-input.png)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit the change">
    Once the new email address is entered, click **Change email**.

    <Note>
      The customer will receive a confirmation email at the new address. The change will not take effect until they confirm it.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If the customer cannot find the confirmation email

Advise the customer to check their **spam, junk, or trash** folder.

<Warning>
  As a last resort only, you may confirm the email change on the customer's behalf.
</Warning>
